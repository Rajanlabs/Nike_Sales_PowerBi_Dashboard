# 👟 Nike Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard designed to analyze **Nike's sales performance, profitability, regional distribution, and product metrics** across multiple retail channels.


---

## 📌 Executive Summary Key Metrics (KPIs)

* **Overall Sales:** $899.90M
* **Total Units Sold:** 2.0M
* **Total Operating Profit:** $332.10M
* **Retailers Count:** 6.
* **Operating Margin %:** 36.91%
* **Average Price per Unit:** $45.22

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights

1. **Operating Margin % by Product (Bar Chart)**
   * **Men's Street Footwear** leads with the highest margin (~40%)[.
   * Followed closely by **Women's Apparel** (38%), **Women's Athletic Footwear** (37%), **Men's Apparel** (36%), **Women's Street Footwear** (35%), and **Men's Athletic Footwear** (34%).

2. **Operating Profit by Region (Bar Chart)**
   * **West Region** generates the highest operating profit ($90M).
   * Order of profit performance: **West** ($90M) > **Northeast** ($68M) > **South** ($61M) / **Southeast** ($61M) > **Midwest** ($53M).

3. **Total Sales by Sales Method (Donut Chart)**
   * **In-store:** $356.6M (39.6%)
   * **Outlet:** $295.6M (32.8%) 
   * **Online:** $247.7M (27.5%) 

4. **Total Sales by Region (Donut Chart)** 
   * **West:** $269.94M (30.00%) 
   * **Northeast:** $186.32M (20.70%) 
   * **Southeast:** $163.17M (18.13%) 
   * **South:** $144.66M (16.08%) 
   * **Midwest:** $135.80M (15.09%) 

5. **Unit Sold by Product (Horizontal Bar Chart)** 
   * **Men's Street Footwear** is the highest volume driver at **0.59M units** .
   * Followed by **Men's Athletic Footwear** (0.44M), **Women's Apparel** (0.43M), **Women's Street Footwear** (0.39M), **Women's Athletic Footwear** (0.32M), and **Men's Apparel** (0.31M) .

6. **Total Sales by Year & Month (Line Chart)** 
   * Tracks monthly sales trends from **Jan 2020 through Oct 2021** .
   * Shows significant sales growth starting from early **2021 onwards** .

7. **Top 10 States by Total Sales (Treemap)** 
   * Highlights leading sales contribution from key states including **New York, California, Florida, Texas, South Carolina, Washington, North Carolina, Virginia, Louisiana, and Hawaii** .
